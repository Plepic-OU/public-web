Direction C

Display font candidates

Design question: Zilla Slab is Mozilla’s brand typeface, so its distinctiveness is borrowed. Voice words: warm, grounded, distinctive; credible and calm; expert without hype. Object frame: a well-made field guide, a letterpress poster in a workshop, not a magazine cover. Reflex picks (Fraunces, Playfair, Lora, Space Grotesk and kin) were screened out before selection; Crete Round and Bree Serif fell in verification, neither ships a 700 weight.
