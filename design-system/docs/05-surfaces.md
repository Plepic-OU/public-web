# Surfaces

Most things are a rule and some space. Use a box only when content must lift off the page.

## Flat by default

- At rest, separate with a 1px or 1.5px border or a tint. A border plus a wide resting shadow is banned.
- Inline code is the only thing that rests on a shadow. Every other shadow is a state: a hover lift, a tooltip, the floating instructor slide, the call-to-action pulse, and the card sheet (`card-shadow-hover`).
- Never same on same: a white panel sits on `bg`, a cream panel on `surface`. `green-surface` marks a favoured option only.

## Judge a flourish at its count

Craft on one specimen is noise across a row. Judge a flourish at the number of times it ships.

## Never

- No side-stripe accent borders.
- No dark surface on any page.
- No cyan, neon, glassmorphism, gradient text, glow or blur.
