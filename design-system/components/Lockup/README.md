# Lockup

The wordmark, the mark and the tagline as one unit, in three arrangements: one row, stacked and mark first.

- Markup: `<a class="logo-lockup">` with `<span class="logo-wordmark">Plepic</span>`, the mark's inline SVG (`.logo-butterfly`) and `<span class="logo-tagline">`, in that order. Add `.logo-lockup--stacked` or `.logo-lockup--mark-first` for the other two arrangements.
- The tagline is two sentence spans; `<i>` marks the green letters, pl and Epic.
- On the web the wordmark is Plepic. For print, merchandise and the social card it is Plepic.com: add `<span style="color: var(--text-2)">.com</span>` inside the wordmark.
- The site header is the one-row lockup without its tagline.
- For files, use the Logos asset group. Rules: The mark section, "The lockup".
