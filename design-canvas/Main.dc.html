Six laws

Why the palette holds these values. A new need takes an existing step, never a new colour.

The 73% rule

Every green is hue 137° at 73% saturation; vivid alone runs at 100%. The accent sits at hue 15°, matched to 73%, so orange and green look mixed by one hand.

One accent per viewport

A CTA button, an urgency badge or an accent dot. Never two: the mobile sticky CTA yields, and states use opacity, never a second orange.

The vivid text ban

--green-vivid is never text on light: 2.5:1 on cream fails AA. It is facet light and decoration.

One mode

Light on every page. No dark section, no dark token, no dark hex; a guard fails any production page that reaches for one.

Headings are ink

At most one green phrase carries the sentence. A fully green heading is off-canon in any medium.

Nothing shines

No cyan, neon, glassmorphism, gradient text, glow or blur. If it could be any company, it is not this one.
