Plepic · Design system

Motion

Everything on this artboard is running. Weight, lift, tilt and breath are the whole register, and each one is a duration and an ease, never a new colour. Hover where it says to.

Four durations

All four dots restart together every 2.4 seconds, so what you are comparing is how soon each one arrives.

--dur-fast150ms

State response: a colour, an opacity, a link waking up

--dur-base300ms

The default. A hover lift, a border appearing

--dur-settle550ms

Something assembling: the crystalline hero, a wing

--dur-entrance700ms

The hero's own arrival, once per page

Two eases, and no third

One for anything that arrives, one for anything that never stops. A linear ease is a bug.

Arriving

--ease-settle · cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)

Fast off the mark, long settle. Every hover, lift, reveal and entrance uses it. It is why the site feels like it is putting things down rather than moving them.

Idling

--ease-calm · ease-in-out

Symmetrical, so a loop has no beginning. Only perpetual motion uses it: the breath, the bob, the antennae.

The reveal

Twenty pixels up and into view over 600ms, staggered 100ms per sibling. Content is visible by default and only hidden once the script has proved it can run, so a failed script shows the page rather than a blank one.

The breath, and the beat

The mark is hinged into three layers: two wings and a core. At rest the wings open to 34deg over 4.6s, a third of a second out of phase, while the core bobs 1.5px. Hover the right-hand one.

At rest. Breath only.

Hover me. The beat opens to 62deg in 1.1s and always wins: it is the last animation named on the layer, so it takes the transform whatever the breath is doing.

Weight and lift

A card rises 5px and prints a sheet beneath it, in 300ms. On a fine pointer it also tilts up to 9deg and 11deg toward the cursor. Hover it.

Rest, then lift. The shadow belongs to the sheet, never to the card.

Reduced motion stops all of it. Every loop, every reveal, every tilt. What it never does is hide something: a reveal that cannot animate shows its content immediately, and the hero holds its from-state fully legible. Motion is the last thing added and the first thing taken away.
