Plepic · Design system

The mark

One locked cut, at every size: twenty-two facets, a leaf body, two antennae and one ember at the head. It is never recoloured, never re-faceted and never redrawn by hand. Copy the geometry, do not rebuild it.

Four host widths, and no fifth

The viewBox owns the ratio, so a placement sizes the host and the mark follows. Only the card glyph is placed today; the other three are declared and waiting, and the header and footer still carry a static copy of the SVG rather than the live component.

Construction

Every number here is in the markup on the page. They are listed so a new placement can be checked, not so it can be retyped.

viewBox0 0 300 280. Square-ish, 300 wide by 280 tall.
Wing facets11 triangles per wing, 22 in total. Adjacent triangles share an edge, so there is no gap to light through.
Centre seamLeft wings end at x=147, right wings start at x=153. The 6px between them is the body channel, and it is why the wings can hinge without clipping each other.
BodyOne bezier path, a leaf from (150,92) to (150,200), filled --green-dark.
AntennaeTwo paths from (150,86) curving out to (136,42) and (164,42). Stroked --green-dark at 1.8px, opacity 0.7.
Tips and headTwo circles r=3.5 at the antenna ends in --green-vivid, and one r=8 at (150,90) in --accent. That ember is the only orange in the mark.
Seam fixEvery polygon is stroked in its own fill at 0.5px. With shape-rendering set on the root it removes the hairline artifacts that otherwise show between adjacent triangles.

The facet law

No two adjacent facets share a colour, so the wing reads as faceted rather than as a shape with texture. The outlines are exact mirrors about the body axis; the asymmetry lives entirely in the fills.

DK · --green-dark B · --green-brand V · --green-vivid

Slots 6 and 7 carry the whole asymmetry: the right wing swaps B and DK where the left does not. It is the one hand-made thing in an otherwise mirrored object, and it is why a wing cannot be produced by flipping the other one in a drawing tool. The generator that built this artboard counts the swapped slots and refuses to build if the number changes.

It breathes

At rest the wings open and close on a long, calm loop while the core bobs. Nothing about the colour changes; breath is rotation and translation only. The Motion artboard runs it.

It beats on arrival

Hovering a card the mark signs makes the wings beat wide and settle. The beat always wins over the breath: it is the last animation named on the layer.

Three layers, one source

The page ships the flat SVG inside a <plepic-mark> host. The script re-stacks what it finds into left wing, right wing and core; it never draws geometry of its own, so the flat mark is always the truth.
