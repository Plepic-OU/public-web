Construction

Every number is in the page’s markup, listed to check a placement, never to retype one.

Wing facets11 triangles per wing, 22 in all, sharing edges so no light shows through.
Centre seamLeft wings end at x=147, right begin at x=153; the 6px channel lets the wings hinge without clipping.
BodyOne bezier leaf from (150,92) to (150,200) in --green-dark.
AntennaeTwo paths from (150,86) to (136,42) and (164,42), --green-dark at 1.8px, opacity 0.7.
Tips and headTwo r=3.5 circles in --green-vivid; one r=8 ember at (150,90) in --accent, the mark’s only orange.
Seam fixEach polygon is stroked in its own fill at 0.5px; with shape-rendering on the root, no hairline shows between triangles.

The facet law

No two adjacent facets share a colour. The outlines mirror exactly; the asymmetry lives in the fills.

It breathes

The wings open and close on a long, calm loop while the core bobs: rotation and translation only, never colour.

It beats on arrival

Hovering a card it signs beats the wings wide on top of the breath, because nested rotations compose.

Three layers, one source
