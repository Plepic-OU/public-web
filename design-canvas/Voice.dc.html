Plepic · Design system

Voice

Words are as fixed as the greens. Write like a practitioner: calm, exact, a little playful, and never longer than the thought.

Who the words are for

Plepic Strategy owns the mission sentence. This is what it asks of a line.

The engineer

becoming an agentic engineer

Show the craft: real code, real checks, the engineer directing and verifying. Never promise the agent does the job.

The entrepreneurial lead

ready to be paid for results, not hours

A founder or team lead. Show the outcome and the arithmetic, and make the relationship worth starting.

The tone

Five qualities, each heard on the live site.

ProfessionalExact nouns and checked numbers. A boast becomes a fact or goes.
A little playfulOne small image or a turn of rhythm; no puns, no exclamation marks. “This page is a cocoon.” (404)
Invites experimentLow-stakes verbs: try, sketch, break, undo. “…we’ll sketch what an agent would do with it.”
GroundedThe engineer is the subject; agents are directed and checked, never partners or minds; your checks decide when the work is done.
InspiringName the after-state, not the tool. “In six weeks you build it into how your team works.”
NeverRevolutionise, 10x, magic, supercharge, autonomy as a selling point, a wellbeing promise.

The tagline decodes the name

PLEPIC is PL(ay) plus EPIC, and each half of the tagline names one half. That is why it cannot be reworded.

PL(ay)EPIC

Curious play. Epic growth.

Bitter italic 500 in --green-brand when given room; in the footer, quiet ink that warms to green on hover. The one line allowed to be green throughout. Estonian pages carry it untranslated, as a signature, because the pun lives in an English name.

The headline pattern

A heading is ink, and at most one phrase inside it carries green. The green phrase is the claim.

This

Practitioners, not trainers

Green marks what the page is about; ink carries the rest. Remove the green and the heading still reads.

Never this

Practitioners, not trainers

A fully green heading emphasises nothing and leaves the next green phrase nothing to earn.

Rules that travel

They hold in a heading, a slide, an email and a proposal.

No em-dashesNot in anything a customer reads. Commas, colons and full stops carry the same joins.
Cohort, not squadOlder records keep the old word; nothing new does.
Sentence, then proofThe supporting line sits in the quieter ink. If it is not quieter, the heading is not carrying.
Never longerA rewrite earns its music by losing words; it never outgrows the line it replaces.

What never enters this canvas. A volatile value lives in the page that states it, where the claims gate can see it.

  • Prices, discounts and any figure with a currency on it
  • Cohort dates, deadlines and anything counted in weeks from today
  • Seat counts, developers trained, ratings, any number that grows
  • Names of people who have not signed
