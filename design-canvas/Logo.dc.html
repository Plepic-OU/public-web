Logo

Wordmark left, butterfly right, half a space between them. The mark is sized to the wordmark’s ascender, not to a number of its own, so the pair scales as one object. Bitter 600 at 1.5rem is the wordmark; nothing else is. The cut was locked on 2026-04-07 and has not moved since.

Two lockups

Three grounds, three wordmarks

The mark itself never changes. Only the wordmark answers the ground it stands on.

Plepic

On light. --green-brand. The default everywhere.

Plepic

On dark. --green-vivid. The brand green goes muddy on ink, so the facet green takes over.

Plepic

On brand. --text. Green on green is never legible, so the wordmark drops to ink.

Never redraw it

Twenty-two facets, one body, two antennae, one ember. The page ships the SVG; nothing hand-draws a polygon, and no module carries a coordinate.

Never recolour it

Not to match a slide, not for a partner deck, not in one colour. A single-colour Plepic butterfly does not exist.

Never stretch it

The viewBox owns the ratio, so size the host and let the mark follow. Four host widths exist and no fifth: 300, 120, 30 and 15px.
