Components

The furniture every page uses. Two buttons, one badge, one panel, one row. Anything a page needs that is not here is a page's own problem and does not become a component until a second page wants it.

Navigation

Training Scopeful Jobs Skill Tree

Two of anything, not five

Two buttons, two eases, two inks that carry text. A third variant has to retire one of the first two.

State, not decoration

A border, a shadow, a surface and a green fill are all states. At rest a component owns as few of them as it can.

The box is optional

Most things are a rule and some space. Reach for a panel only when the content genuinely has to be lifted off the page.
