Navigation

Quieter ink at rest, full ink on hover, green and underlined for the page you are on. An external link earns an arrow and nothing else.

Sizes and variants are orthogonal

Three by three, not nine components. A variant that changes the box is a new size pretending to be a colour.

State, not decoration

A border, a shadow, a surface and a green fill are all states. At rest a component owns as few of them as it can.

The box is optional

Most things are a rule and some space. Reach for a panel only when the content genuinely has to be lifted off the page.
