Standing figure

No window, no frame: the person is cut out and stands directly on a flat brand-green field that runs off all four edges of the card. The bet is that a real photograph standing in a coloured space carries the whole card, and that the green does the identity work the shards were doing badly.

The field is --green-surface bled to all four edges, and the photograph sits on it with mix-blend-mode: multiply, so the studio white becomes the field with no mask and the transparent PNG needs nothing at all. Resting elevation is a 2px ink contact shadow under an 18px ambient, at 6 and 7 percent; hover lifts the card 14px and opens that into a 24px and a 54px layer; the edge is not a border but an inset hairline of brand green at 12 percent, which is the faintest thing that still holds a mint field against the cream page. Everything the card says lives on one white plate inset on three sides so the figure passes behind it and is cropped by the card rather than by the caption, the foot plate is dropped because a second collector line would need a second plate over the same body, and the type line is printed on the field at the top instead, where all three frames are empty anyway and the 16px it gives back to the plate is what lifts that plate off Vootele's shoulders. Stated as literals because the system has no token for them: the 5:7 playing-card aspect, a tilt softened to 9 and 11 degrees at 1400px perspective (a rotated photograph of a face skews where a rotated illustration does not), and three per-portrait framing triples measured off the images so every head lands at one size and one height. On the risk: the multiply was measured, not assumed, and it is clean, since the field scales colour by 0.93 red, 0.99 green, 0.95 blue, so Vootele's navy and Kaido's teal keep their fabric and lose about three units of red; sampling the rendered cards found the field at exactly #edfcf1 inside the photo, no seam at the image edge, and a 2px anti-aliased transition at the hair line rather than the grey halo the JPEG near-whites (225 in kaido.jpg, 232 in vootele.jpg) predicted.
