The colophon is gone and its rule now closes the column on the day-job line, and the square becomes a portrait so the two rules bracket a picture instead of a label: a 5:6 plate at the full width of the measure, each shot multiplied onto --bg-alt so that three studio whites land on exactly one ground, then saturate(0.8) contrast(1.12) applied identically to all three and nothing graded per person. What lets the colour in is the crop and not the grade: at 288 square the garment was a third of the picture, and at 288 by 346 cropped to one head size and one eye line it is a band at the foot, so the hue is carried by skin, which these three men actually share. Honest read: the three grounds measure identical at 243,239,231 and the three eye lines sit on one guide, so the set holds at the faces, but it does not hold at the clothes, where a yellow shirt, a teal jersey and a navy tee stay three colours nobody chose together, and 0.8 is as far as they can be pulled together before the faces go ashen. Vootele is the weak card and the crop is why: his source is 512px and this frame enlarges it about 1.7 times, so at 2x he is visibly the softest of the three, and no grade repairs that.
