The card is a field of --green-surface behind a white window, and both polarities were rendered before this one was kept: Kaido and Vootele carry a pure white ground baked into their JPEGs, so a green window shows each of them inside his own white rectangle while the transparent Joosep sits on green, and only white makes the three one set. The edge is a 1px --green-brand hairline at 26 percent, and the elevation is a contact shadow at rest, 0 1px 2px at 5 percent ink, that opens to 0 22px 38px -16px at 28 percent as the card rises the shipped --lift-height; the tilt caps are halved to 7 and 9 degrees on the seat, because a mounted print tips and does not flop. The photographs are untouched colour, no filter and no duotone, because the set is made by geometry alone: each frame is scaled until the heads match and shifted until every head top lands on one line, which the render holds across the three to within half a percentage point of the window height. The system has no token for a hairline tint, a soft shadow or a per portrait offset, so --st-hairline, --st-contact, --st-lifted, --st-win and the three --p-* triples are literals in this block, and the type line and the foot plate are gone, folded into the one rail at the top, because a card whose argument is margin cannot afford small caps at both ends. Below 700px the stamp lies down instead of shrinking: the window becomes a 98px square at the left of a 128px card, against 275px inside a 432px card standing up, the mount stays proportionally wider lying down than it is standing up, and the denomination moves to the corner of the rail, which is where a stamp prints its value anyway.
