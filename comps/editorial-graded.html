The photographs keep their colour and get one correction, written once on one selector and applied to all three: saturate(0.58) sepia(0.18) contrast(1.07) brightness(1.04), still multiplied onto a --bg-alt plate, so the paper's own warm cast is the last step of the grade and the one step that is identical by construction. Every number answers a measurement taken on the rendered plates, not a preference: saturate stops at 0.58 because that is where the 90th-percentile chroma spread across the set is exactly halved, 0.108 to 0.054; Kaido's frame crosses from net-cool to net-warm just past sepia 0.14, so 0.18 clears the threshold with margin and puts all three median hues at 43, 43 and 26 degrees; and brightness 1.04 cancels what multiplying against #f3efe7 costs and clips the studio white back to the plate tone now that grayscale is gone, which the plate corners confirm at #f3efe7 on all three. contrast is the smallest of the three correcting moves on purpose, because it is the only one that pays: the lift widens luma spread between the bright photograph and the dark one from 0.176 to 0.189, a fair price for not going milky beside an ink rule at 1.07 and not a fair one at 1.20. It gets most of the way and there is one place it does not: the three share a white balance now, the yellow shirt has come down from shouting to speaking and the teal jersey has come back as a green that belongs on this page, but a multiplicative chain compresses differences and cannot close ratios, so Joosep's frame carries 2.1 times Vootele's mean chroma where it carried 1.8 before, and still averages more than twice his luma, which is why the eye lands on Joosep first and no single chain can change that. Literals the system has no token for, all named where they sit: the four grade values, the 1.5px rule weight, the 24px sheet bleed, the 1.4rem byline, the 0.16em mono tracking, the 5px lift and its two shadows, the three tilt values, and the per-person crops, which stay per-person because framing is geometry and the grade is the thing that is not allowed to be.
