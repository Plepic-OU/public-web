## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not AI slop.** A visitor asks "how was this made?", not "which AI made this?" The single-hue green-on-cream palette, Zilla Slab slab-serif, the crystalline low-poly butterfly, asymmetric badge corners, and `while(task) { think -> act -> observe }` pseudocode chips form a specific, non-generic identity that passes brand.md's slop tests and both PRODUCT.md anti-references. The lane is coherent (warm-technical, "museum-caption-meets-terminal"), not editorial-magazine drift, not Stripe-beige. Proof-over-claim is executed, not sloganed: named clients, visible Tootukassa subsidy arithmetic (2,520 -> -2,016 -> 504), a live-contrast public design system, real instructor photos, transparent hiring pay.

**Deterministic scan (Assessment B):** CLI = 8 findings, ALL known false positives (single-font x6: fonts load via HTML `<link>`, invisible to CSS scan; numbered-section-markers on scopeful: a real 01/02/03 product sequence; overused-font on 404: Plus Jakarta Sans is the locked brand body font). Zero real CLI findings. Runtime deterministic checks: 0 contrast fails, 0 ghost-card, 0 side-stripe across homepage/training/scopeful. Line-length flags all container-width FPs on centered captions. Only marginal real signal: 2 footer tap-targets < 44px on mobile (low severity). Overlay injection blocked by the page's own production CSP (whitelists self + Google only) — correct hygiene, reported as fallback.

**Two stale findings from the 2026-06-11 critique, corrected by live inspection:**
- Homepage butterfly at `opacity: 0.28` is NOT a defect. DESIGN.md explicitly sanctions "the complete solid mark as an ambient watermark at a low group opacity (the hero)." The ban is on per-facet fill opacity / outline-only wings, not low group opacity on the whole solid mark. Drop this finding.
- "Inner pages center everything / asymmetric grammar homepage-only" is PARTIALLY stale. Scopeful and claude-code *heroes* are asymmetric (1.4fr/0.6fr). Inner section *bodies* (curriculum, workflow, pricing) do center, but the heroes hold the doctrine.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Training hero is built from the disowned sci-fi "mech/HUD" era, on the money page.** Verified live + in source: training hero DOM is `.mech-viewport` -> `.mech-image-frame`/`.mech-image-inner`/`.mech-hud-stats` wrapping 4x `.stat-card`. PRODUCT.md names "the pre-2026-03 sci-fi mech look" and "hero-metric templates" as anti-references to NEVER reintroduce; DESIGN.md Section 7 says "Don't add hero-metric stat-card templates." The site's own CI guard `no mech-* legacy class names` exists but is `test.fixme('...')` with the comment "Enable after the training-hero stat cards are recast (critique P1)." Known, ticketed, still-open, on the page ads land on. The accompanying `agentic-harness.webp` illustration's glowing teal-green mesh nodes read as the banned "glow" at viewing size (objectively green-dominant, ~0.1% cyan pixels, but the bloom reads sci-fi). Fix: recast the four stats into sanctioned bordered-panel/mono-spec grammar, rename the `.mech-*` family, re-flatten or replace the harness illustration's node glow, then remove `.fixme` to enable the guard. Command: `$impeccable layout` (recast) then `$impeccable harden` (enable guard).

2. **[P1] The conversion hand-off drops 100% of brand identity.** All registration CTAs -> a bare `forms.gle` Google Form; all "talk" CTAs -> a bare `calendar.app.google` page. No logo, cream, butterfly, Zilla headline, price recap, or reassurance survives the click. Peak-end rule violated at the exact commitment moment: a 504-2,520/dev decision completes on stock Google chrome, eroding the trust the whole site built. This is the single highest-leverage emotional fix. Fix: (a) add CTA micro-copy so the leap is expected ("Opens a short Google Form, 2 minutes"); (b) brand the Google Form theme (Plepic color + logo header) and prefill where possible; (c) consider a lightweight branded confirmation/interstitial before/after the external surface. Command: `$impeccable clarify` (micro-copy) + a branding pass on the Form.

3. **[P2] Four CTA labels, one destination.** "Book a Hackathon for Your Team", "Book a 30-minute call", and 2x "Talk to Kaido" all resolve to the identical calendar link. Label-promise mismatch; a hackathon-interested lead lands on a generic call booker; the skeptical CTO reads it as funnel-optimization theater. Fix: differentiate destinations (a hackathon-specific event type/form) OR converge to one honest phrase per intent. Command: `$impeccable clarify`.

4. **[P3] 404 is a navigational dead-end.** No header/footer; only 3 quick links. Team, Jobs, Skill Tree, and all contact paths unreachable. Fix: include the standard nav header (or footer) on 404. Command: `$impeccable harden`.

5. **[P3] Mobile training stat row is cramped.** 4 stat blocks across at 390px force multi-line wraps ("MAX PARTICIPANTS", "AVG. TRAINING SCORE"). Fix: 2x2 grid below ~420px, or 3 strongest stats on mobile. (Resolves naturally if P1 recast happens.) Command: `$impeccable adapt`.

## Questions to Consider
1. If the design system is a credibility weapon, why does the buyer's single most important moment (clicking Reserve/Book) happen on a surface with zero design system applied?
2. Four-labels-one-link: are you A/B testing which verb converts, or haven't you differentiated the offerings' funnels? If A/B, is the trust cost of a skeptical CTO noticing worth the signal?
3. The mech/HUD legacy is a known P1 with a disabled guard test waiting on it. What's blocking the recast, the illustration asset, layout risk on the money page, or priority? Enabling that one guard test is the cheapest way to make "we don't reintroduce the sci-fi era" true instead of aspirational.
