Before you book

The program

What is agentic coding?

Agentic coding is a software development approach where an LLM uses tools in a loop to plan, write, test, and refine code autonomously. Unlike single-prompt AI, an agentic system iterates until the goal is achieved. We teach it on Claude Code, a terminal agent with full system access, while Copilot and Cursor stay inside the editor.

Who is Plepic’s training designed for?

Senior engineers with 2+ years of professional experience and access to a real codebase. The work agencies, product companies, and AI labs are now hiring for: walking into unfamiliar code, partnering with AI agents, shipping outcomes. Not for learning to code.

How long does the training program take?

The training runs over six Fridays, each a full day (9:00–15:30) via Google Meet. The program covers 50 academic hours; participants apply what they learn in their own codebases between sessions.

Is there a certificate at the end?

Yes. Participants who complete the program receive a Plepic Certificate of Completion in Agentic Development. It covers Claude Code, context management, and the full agentic loop.

Is this too basic for a team that already uses agents?

No. We teach the method, not the tool. Later sessions cover specs an agent can verify, verification gates, parallel agents in git worktrees, observability and agent-native architecture. Prior Claude experience helps, but it is not required. The entry bar is 2+ years of software development and access to a real codebase.

Pricing & subsidy

How much does it cost, and how does the Estonian subsidy work?

€2,520 +VAT per developer. For Estonian employers, Töötukassa reimburses up to 80% of the training invoice including VAT, capped at €2,500 per employee over three years; the employer applies in e-töötukassa before training starts and we prepare the application with them. Plepic koolitus (Plepic OÜ, reg 11275881) is the registered training provider, EHIS majandustegevusteade nr 260952, and the program meets Töötukassa’s minimum of 50 academic hours.

Is the training available outside Estonia?

Yes. The training runs online via Google Meet, in English, open to European teams at the standard price of €2,520 per developer. The Estonian state subsidy applies only to Estonian-registered employers.
